## TLS dependency correction — researched before change

XERJ query `ClientIdentity --symbol` in `ref-mysql_async` returned both native and rustls identity implementations at pinned `d7525dcb1d35f3d60101a2e95e84171c3d3ac4a5`. Read `src/opts/rustls_opts.rs:15–85` and its adjacent PKCS#1/PKCS#8/SEC1 and missing-key tests at lines 195–225. Native TLS accepts a PKCS#12 archive, whereas the rustls identity accepts our configured PEM certificate/key pair. Independently checked the downloaded `mysql_async` 0.37.1 Cargo manifest: the supported features are `rustls-tls`, `ring`, and `tls12`; `rustls-tls-ring` is not defined. Adaptation: keep `minimal-rust` and use those three supported TLS features for MySQL, while retaining native TLS for PostgreSQL. T03 must prove trusted/untrusted CA, hostname rejection and mutual TLS against real servers before accepting the driver choice. The initial native TLS Cargo check passed; it did not prove PEM identity support.
