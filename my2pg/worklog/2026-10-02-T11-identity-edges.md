# 2026-10-02 — T11 unsigned maximum and generation-policy edges

Status: focused native coverage slice; T11 remains open. Ownership: new `tests/cases/t11_identity_edges.rs` only. Coordinator owns test registration, task status, and shared XERJ indexes.

## Contract and acceptance

The unsigned BIGINT *data* range is supported as PostgreSQL `numeric(20,0)` through `2^64-1`; its AUTO_INCREMENT generator remains unsupported because PostgreSQL sequence types are signed and the planner has no alternative generator policy. Source `AUTO_INCREMENT_INCREMENT` or `AUTO_INCREMENT_OFFSET` other than `(1,1)` is currently a blocking policy diagnostic whenever a selected table has an AUTO_INCREMENT column. This slice will verify both behaviors through the real runner: exact maximum-value round-trip without AUTO_INCREMENT, and `IDENTITY_INCREMENT_OFFSET` refusal before target schema creation after changing the disposable MySQL server's global setting. The test must restore the original global settings even when the runner reports refusal.
